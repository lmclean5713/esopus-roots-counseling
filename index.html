About Me

Kelly is a Licensed Mental Health Counselor, Certified School Counselor, and National Certified Counselor who works with children, adolescents, and adults. She specializes in OCD, ADHD, and anxiety-related disorders, using a primarily Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) approach informed by developmental and attachment-based perspectives.

Kelly views symptoms as meaningful signals and works collaboratively with clients to understand underlying patterns, develop effective coping skills, and build greater flexibility, self-trust, and emotional balance. Her style is warm, thoughtful, and relational, with an emphasis on both practical skill-building and deeper self-understanding.

She has extensive experience supporting children and adolescents, as well as adults navigating anxiety, burnout, identity development, and life transitions.

Outside of work, Kelly enjoys spending time outdoors, creative activities, making music and performing with her band, and spending time with her cat and dog.
