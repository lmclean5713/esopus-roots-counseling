Learn more about Individual Therapy

Kelly provides individualized counseling to children, adolescents, and adults in a supportive, collaborative environment. She works with clients to better understand their thoughts, emotions, and behavioral patterns while developing practical strategies to navigate challenges and build greater confidence and self-trust. Kelly specializes in OCD, ADHD, anxiety, and depression, and also supports clients experiencing burnout, identity development, relationship challenges, and life transitions. Her approach is primarily grounded in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and informed by developmental and attachment-based perspectives, allowing her to tailor treatment to each client's unique needs, experiences, and goals.
