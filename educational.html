What We Offer

Master Scheduling

Collaboration with school leadership to develop efficient master schedules that balance student needs, course offerings, staffing, resources, and school-specific requirements.

Student and Community-Centered Planning

A scheduling approach that weighs logistical demands alongside the impact on students, teachers, and the broader school community, so schedules are practical and responsive to the school's needs.

Individualized Educational Consulting

Tailored consulting for schools that draws on both counseling and school-based experience, supported by past work on master scheduling at several public schools and as Master Scheduling Lead at Williamsburg Charter High School.
